The honest answer to "which computer-use agent is best" is: it depends on the task. Claude Cowork is strongest for local documents and files, OpenAI Operator for web tasks like booking and forms, Manus for hands-off autonomous research, and Sai for a guided cloud workspace that asks before acting. Pick by what you actually need done.

## What "computer use" means

A computer-use agent is an AI that operates a computer the way you do — it can open applications, move the mouse, click, type, and read the screen — rather than just answering questions. That's the leap from a chatbot that *describes* how to do something to an agent that *does* it: logs into a portal, works through forty records, fills a form, and hands you the result.

Because the category is new, the tools are easy to confuse. They're not interchangeable, and the wrong pick wastes a subscription. Here's the lay of the land.

## How to choose

Start from the task, not the brand:

- **Documents and files on your machine?** Go with Claude Cowork. It's repeatedly called out as the strongest at local file operations, from reading PDFs to formatting Word, Excel, and PowerPoint without mangling the layout.
- **Web tasks — bookings, forms, filling portals?** OpenAI Operator is built for cross-site work where you'd otherwise click through a dozen pages.
- **Long, hands-off research projects?** Manus runs autonomously and suits non-technical users who want to set a goal and come back to a finished result.
- **Want to watch and approve as it works?** Sai runs in a secure cloud workspace and asks for your approval before any critical action, which makes it the gentlest on-ramp if you're nervous about letting an agent act.

## The one question that matters more than the brand

Every comparison eventually lands on the same risk: **how much can this agent do without asking, and can you take it back?** A cloud agent that runs on its own is powerful but opaque; an agent that runs locally and only touches what you grant is easier to trust with your real files and accounts.

That's the design axis to optimize for, and it applies no matter which vendor you pick. Before you hand an agent your credentials or filesystem, confirm three things: what it can reach, whether it pauses for your approval on consequential actions, and how to revoke access instantly. The best tool is the one you understand well enough to stop.

## Takeaway

Computer-use agents are finally real, and they're genuinely different tools rather than the same product with different logos. Match the agent to the task — files to Cowork, web to Operator, autonomy to Manus, oversight to Sai — and anchor every choice in the control question above.
